THE GAME

ABOUT ASTRO LOOP

A small ship. An endless stream of trouble. No spare lives.

ASTRO LOOP: ENDLESS is a one-hit 2D space shooter. Guide your ship across the bottom of the screen, shoot down incoming enemies, and dodge whatever they send your way.

Each enemy you defeat adds to your score. New enemy types appear as the round continues, so surviving gets trickier the longer you last. There is no win screen to reach: the run ends when your ship gets hit.
