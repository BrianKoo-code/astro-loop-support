ASTRO LOOP: ENDLESS / PRIVACY

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ASTRO LOOP: ENDLESS (the "Game"), an independent game released under the name Wasted Unicorn, handles information.

01 / INFORMATION IN THE GAME

The Game does not require an account or sign-in. It does not ask players to provide a name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location to play.

Your best score is stored locally on your device. It is not intentionally sent to the developer by the Game.

02 / ADVERTISING AND ANALYTICS

The current release has no in-game advertising or dedicated advertising or analytics integrations. The developer does not sell personal information or use player data for targeted advertising.

03 / TECHNICAL AND PLATFORM DATA

The Game is built with the Unity engine and distributed through Apple's App Store. Depending on the Game's diagnostics configuration and device settings, Apple or Unity may process technical information such as crash reports, error logs, or device information.

We do not use technical diagnostics for targeted advertising or behavioral profiling.

04 / SUPPORT EMAIL

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to questions and troubleshoot issues.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, unless a longer period is required by law.

05 / YOUR CHOICES

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of information you have sent to support, subject to applicable legal obligations.

You can remove locally stored game data by deleting the Game and its saved data from your device, subject to device settings and backups.

06 / POLICY UPDATES

If the Game's features or data practices change, including the addition of advertisements, analytics, or other services, we will update this Privacy Policy and the related App Store disclosures as appropriate.

07 / CONTACT
